### Scripture
“I have been crucified with Christ and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I now live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me.” 

**– Galatians 2:20**

### Reflection
I was reading about Karl Barth, a pastor in Switzerland in the 1930s.  Barth has this great phrase: “to be a Christian is to be called to Christ with others.” Faith is not a solitary pursuit but a communal one. We are called to act not in isolation, but in community, accountable to something greater than ourselves.  

Barth warns that we should not act out of a sense that we are the moral exemplars or leaders. After all, that is what the Pharisees thought of themselves.  Instead, we should always be careful to reflect the teachings of Jesus. We don’t _just_ stand up for good or against evil in the world because we think it’s right or wrong. We stand up because that’s what Jesus would have done.  That is what he did, on the cross, for us.  And for all of us who were saved by that act, we are called by that example to act both individually and together, in community, to live a right life and stand for good in the world.

### Prayer
Dear Lord, we thank You for the opportunity to serve Your church, and we ask for the strength to serve with joy and commitment. Help us to remember that our service is not a burden but a privilege, and that it is through serving You that we find true fulfillment. Fill our hearts with gladness as we work together in Your name. May our actions reflect the love and commitment we have for You and for the congregation. Grant us the strength to persevere in our responsibilities, that our service glorify You in all things.
