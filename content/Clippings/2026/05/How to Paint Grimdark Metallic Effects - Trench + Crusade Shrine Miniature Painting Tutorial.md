In this video I show my process of painting an Anchorite Shrine from Trench Crusade. This miniature was printed using an Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra and painted with a variety of Army Painter, Vallejo and Pro Acryl paints. I'm trying to replicate the grimdark style the lore is known for without smothering the miniatures with grime or enamel washes. In addition to the acrylics I added some weathering Verdigris / Rust effects from the Goblins Hut Dirty Down range.
